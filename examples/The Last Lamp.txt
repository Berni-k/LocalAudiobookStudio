Chapter 2: The Letter

On her next visit, Clara brought a letter with a blue seal. Tobias read it twice at the kitchen table.

[pause 1.5s]

[Clara] What does it say?

[Tobias] It says the lighthouse is getting an electric lamp. It will switch itself on at dusk and off at dawn.

[Clara] Then who will look after it?

[Tobias] Nobody, little one. That's rather the point.

[pause 1s]

He folded the letter carefully and put it in his coat pocket. That evening he climbed the stairs more slowly than usual, counting every step, the way he had on his very first night.

Chapter 3: The Last Night

The workmen were due on Monday. On Sunday night, a storm came in from the north.

Far out on the water, a small fishing boat was fighting its way home. On board, Mack Doyle could see nothing but rain and black waves.

[Mack] Come on, old man. Where's your light?

[pause 800ms]

Then, through the rain, the beam swept over him, steady and bright, the way it had for forty years.

[Mack] There you are. There you are.

At dawn, the storm was gone. On the quay, the fishermen were waiting, and Clara was waiting with them.

[Mack] You kept us alive last night, Tobias.

[Tobias] I only lit a lamp.

They did not say much after that. They simply took off their caps, one by one, as the old keeper walked past. And for the rest of his life, whenever Tobias looked out to sea at night and saw the new light turning, he would smile, because he knew it was still his.
